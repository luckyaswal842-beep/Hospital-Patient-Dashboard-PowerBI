# Hospital-Patient-Dashboard-PowerBI
An interactive hospital analytics dashboard designed to track patient details, billing, and treatment patterns, enabling healthcare providers to improve decision-making, optimize costs, and enhance patient care.
## 📌 Project Overview

Efficient patient management and cost tracking are critical in healthcare. This project analyzes hospital data to provide insights into patient information, billing, treatment timelines, and resource utilization.

The dashboard helps stakeholders monitor patient status, understand financial distribution, and track treatment activities over time.

## 🎯 Objectives
- Monitor patient details and admission records
- Analyze hospital charges and billing patterns
- Track treatment and medication trends
- Provide insights for better hospital management

## 📂 Dataset
Source: Hospital dataset from a YouTube tutorial 
- Key Features:
- Patient Name
- Age & Gender
- Diagnosis
- Admission & Discharge Date
- Billing & Charges
- Medication tracking

- <a href="https://github.com/luckyaswal842-beep/Hospital-Patient-Dashboard-PowerBI/blob/main/Hospital-DataAnalysis-Dataset.pbix">Dataset

## 🧹 Data Cleaning & Preparation
- Handled missing/null values
- Standardized date formats (admission & discharge)
- Cleaned categorical fields (gender, diagnosis)
- Created calculated fields:
- Total bill
 -Paid amount
 - Length of stay (admission → discharge)

## 🧰 Tools & Skills Used
- Tool: Power BI  
- Data Cleaning & Transformation
- Data Visualization
- DAX / Calculated Measures
- Healthcare Data Analysis

## 🔍 Key Insights
- Majority of hospital charges come from a single major cost category (~74%)
- Patient stayed for 7 days, indicating moderate treatment duration
- Small gap between total bill and paid amount, showing efficient billing recovery
- Medication activity varies across days and months, indicating treatment intensity patterns

## 💡 Recommendations
- Optimize high-cost components to reduce overall billing burden
- Monitor patients with longer stays for cost and resource efficiency
- Improve tracking of medication usage for better treatment planning
- Enhance billing transparency to ensure full payment collection

## 🎯 Business Impact

This dashboard enables hospitals to:
- Improve patient management efficiency
- Track and optimize healthcare costs
- Enhance billing and revenue tracking
- Support data-driven healthcare decisions
